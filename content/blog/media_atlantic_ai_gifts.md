Victoria Feng interviewed me for [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/11/ai-agents-holiday-shopping-muse/689587/) on people who let AI agents choose and buy their presents.

I appear twice. Once as an enthusiast: I asked my girlfriend's Muse agent, which knows what she likes and saves on Instagram, to find a hotel that matched her taste. And once as a cautionary tale: my own agent (named Hermenegildo) ordered her flowers and signed the card with his name instead of mine.
