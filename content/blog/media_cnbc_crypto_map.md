CNBC quoted me in [This map shows where cryptocurrency is taking off around the world](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/08/18/new-cryptocurrency-bitcoin-user-global-map.html) on the Chainalysis 2021 adoption index. I pointed out that many of the top countries have capital controls or large emigrant populations, and that the methodology has a huge blind spot in sanctioned countries such as Cuba, where peer-to-peer data is poor.
