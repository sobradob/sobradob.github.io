I moderated this panel on the MoneyConf stage at Web Summit in Lisbon, with Juan Pablo Ortega (co-founder and CEO of Yuno) and Gabriel Le Roux (co-founder and CEO of Primer). We discussed why a payment stack that looks unified to the consumer is not unified for the merchant, and what AI agents will pay with.
