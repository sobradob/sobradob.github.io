I interviewed Guilherme Murtinho (founding partner and VP Europe at Transfero Group) on the MoneyConf stage at Web Summit in Lisbon. We discussed how a Brazilian importer can pay a South Korean supplier from a Pix transfer, what the Brazilian central bank's new framework changes, and whether local stablecoins have a future next to USDT and USDC.
