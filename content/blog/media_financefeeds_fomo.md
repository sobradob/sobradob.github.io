I wrote [a piece for FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/fomo-no-paid-ads-creators-half-its-users/) on how Fomo, a crypto trading app that raised a \$75m Series B in June 2026, said it grew without paid ads (they reportedly began this September). A co-founder said creators once brought in about 50% of its users, a share that is now closer to 15%.

It covers the clipping marketplace that pays around \$1 per 1,000 views, the agencies and rosters on top of it, and how often the promotion goes undisclosed: of the 45 YouTube videos about Fomo in the sample, 42 had a referral link and 25 of those had no disclosure wording.

Also available [in Italian](https://financefeeds.com/it/fomo-niente-annunci-pagati-creatori-meta-utenti/).
