In 2026 my Forbes reporting was cited in these official documents:

- **US House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform**, 22 May 2026. Chairman James Comer's [letter to Kalshi CEO Tarek Mansour](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Kalshi-Prediction-Markets-Letter.pdf) on insider trading in prediction markets quotes my piece "Third Largest Economy: $5.9 Trillion Gambling Beyond Regulation" (footnote 8).
- **US Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs**, 13 May 2026. Ranking Member Elizabeth Warren's [letter to SEC Chair Paul Atkins](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20260513lettertosecrewlfdolomite.pdf) asking for an investigation into World Liberty Financial's $75 million loan cites my piece "Don't Be Exit Liquidity: Trump's WLFI Borrows $75M On Own Token" (footnote 11).
- **US House Committee on Agriculture**, 16 April 2026. The [hearing record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119hhrg64650/pdf/CHRG-119hhrg64650.pdf) for CFTC Chairman Michael Selig includes a statement from the Indian Gaming Association that cites my piece "Prediction Markets Are The Super Bowl's Breakout Winners" (page 114, footnote 4).
- **CFTC rulemaking on prediction markets**, 30 April 2026. A [public comment](https://comments.cftc.gov/Handlers/PdfHandler.ashx?id=36050) by Professor Daniel J. Taylor, Director of the Wharton Forensic Analytics Lab, cites my piece "Every Bet Wins: Prediction Market Insiders Trace Back To India" (footnote 5).
- **SEC Crypto Task Force**, 22 January 2026. A [written submission](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-written-james-overdahl-tokenized-us-equities-01-22-2026.pdf) by former SEC Chief Economist James Overdahl on tokenised US equities quotes my piece "Locked Out And Liquidated: Traders Blame Binance For $19 Billion Crash" (footnote 64).
